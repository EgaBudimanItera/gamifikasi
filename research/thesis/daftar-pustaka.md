# Daftar Pustaka

## Referensi Utama

### Gamifikasi dalam Pendidikan

1. **Deterding, S., Dixon, D., Khaled, R., & Nacke, L. (2011).** "From Game Design Elements to Gamefulness: Defining Gamification." _Proceedings of the 15th International Academic MindTrek Conference_, 9–15.
   - Link: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2181037.2181040
   - Konsep dasar definisi gamifikasi dan elemen desain game.

2. **Saleem, A. N., Noori, N. M., & Ozdamli, F. (2022).** "Gamification Applications in E-Learning: A Literature Review." _Technology, Knowledge and Learning_, 27(1), 139–159.
   - Link: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10758-020-09487-x
   - Systematic review: elemen gamifikasi paling umum (points, leaderboards, badges, level) dan dampaknya pada e-learning.

3. **Lampropoulos, G., & Sidiropoulos, A. (2024).** "Impact of Gamification on Students' Learning Outcomes and Academic Performance: A Longitudinal Study." _Education Sciences_, 14(4), 367.
   - Link: https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7102/14/4/367
   - Studi longitudinal 3 tahun membandingkan gamified vs traditional vs online learning.

4. **Fiş Erümit, S., & Karakuş Yılmaz, T. (2022).** "Gamification Design in Education: What Might Give a Sense of Play and Learning?" _Technology, Knowledge and Learning_, 27, 1039–1061.
   - Link: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10758-022-09604-y
   - Design-based research: elemen gamifikasi untuk meningkatkan motivasi dan partisipasi aktif.

6. **Kode, O. (2025).** "Gamification in Education: Review of Challenges and Recommendations for Effective Practice." _International Journal on Cybernetics & Informatics_, 14(4), 13–28.
   - Link: https://ijcionline.com/paper/14/14425ijci02.pdf
   - Review tantangan dan rekomendasi implementasi gamifikasi.

### Requirement Engineering & User Story

7. **Lucassen, G., Dalpiaz, F., Van der Werf, J. M. E., & Brinkkemper, S. (2016).** "Improving Agile Requirements: The Quality User Story Framework and Tool." _Requirements Engineering_, 21, 383–400.
   - Link: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00766-015-0230-x
   - Quality User Story (QUS) framework dengan 13 kriteria kualitas.

8. **Raharjana, I. K., Siahaan, D., & Fatichah, C. (2023).** "User Story Extraction from Natural Language for Requirements Elicitation." _Journal of Systems and Software_, 200, 111634.
   - Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0950584923000496
   - Ekstraksi user story dari teks natural language untuk elicitation requirements.

9. **Li, Y., Keung, J., Yang, Z., Ma, X., Zhang, J., & Liu, S. (2024).** "SimAC: Simulating Agile Collaboration to Generate Acceptance Criteria in User Story Elaboration." _Automated Software Engineering_, 31, 55.
   - Link: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10515-024-00448-7
   - Simulasi kolaborasi agile untuk menghasilkan acceptance criteria.

10. **Hendriana, A., Raharjo, T., & Nurfitriani, A. (2024).** "Approaches in Determining User Story Quality through Requirement Elicitation: A Systematic Literature Review." _Indonesian Journal of Computer Science_, 12(6), 3599.
   - Link: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/377227381
   - SLR tentang pendekatan penentuan kualitas user story.

### Behavior-Driven Development (BDD)

11. **Nascimento, N., Santos, A. R., Sales, A., & Chanin, R. (2020).** "Behavior-Driven Development: A Case Study on Its Impacts on Agile Development Teams." _Proceedings of the IEEE/ACM 42nd International Conference on Software Engineering Workshops_, 109–116.
    - Link: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3387940.3391480
    - Studi kasus dampak BDD pada tim pengembangan agile.

12. **Soares, M. S. (2024).** "Using Behavior-Driven Development (BDD) for Non-Functional Requirements." _Software_, 3(3), 271–283.
    - Link: https://www.mdpi.com/2674-113X/3/3/14
    - Penerapan BDD untuk non-functional requirements (ISO/IEC 25010).

13. **García, M. et al. (2023).** "Behaviour Driven Development: A Systematic Mapping Study." _Journal of Systems and Software_, 203, 111744.
    - Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0164121223001449
    - Systematic mapping study tentang BDD dari 2006–2021 dengan 166 papers.

### Design Science Research (DSR)

14. **Peffers, K., Tuunanen, T., Rothenberger, M. A., & Chatterjee, S. (2007).** "A Design Science Research Methodology for Information Systems Research." _Journal of Management Information Systems_, 24(3), 45–77.
    - Link: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.2753/MIS0742-1222240302
    - Metodologi DSR dengan 6 langkah: problem identification, objectives, design & development, demonstration, evaluation, communication.

15. **Hevner, A. R., March, S. T., Park, J., & Ram, S. (2004).** "Design Science in Information Systems Research." _MIS Quarterly_, 28(1), 75–105.
    - Link: https://www.jstor.org/stable/25148625
    - Framework DSR untuk Information Systems research.

16. **Baskerville, R., Pries-Heje, J., & Venable, J. R. (2026).** "MEDS: Methodology for Evaluation in Design Science." _European Journal of Information Systems_.
    - Link: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/0960085X.2026.2627280
    - Metode evaluasi untuk DSR (MuSCoW, Evaluation Selection Framework).

### Basis Data & Model Relasional

17. **Codd, E. F. (1970).** "A Relational Model of Data for Large Shared Data Banks." _Communications of the ACM_, 13(6), 377–387.
    - Link: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/362384.362685
    - Konsep dasar normalisasi database dan model relasional.

### Kompetisi Peer-to-Peer dalam Pendidikan

17. **Topping, K. J. (2005).** "Trends in Peer Learning." _Educational Psychology_, 25(6), 631–645.
    - Link: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01443410500344168
    - Peer tutoring dan kompetisi meningkatkan hasil belajar hingga 0.4 SD.

18. **Roediger, H. L., & Butler, A. C. (2011).** "The Critical Role of Retrieval Practice in Long-Term Retention." _Trends in Cognitive Sciences_, 15(1), 20–27.
    - Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1364661310002350
    - Active recall lebih efektif dari re-reading untuk retensi jangka panjang.

19. **Festinger, L. (1954).** "A Theory of Social Comparison Processes." _Human Relations_, 7(2), 117–140.
    - Link: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/001872675400700202
    - Teori komparasi sosial sebagai dasar motivasi kompetisi.
